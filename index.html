GroomSome

Every animal, remembered.

Milo is happy and wriggly, needs a second pair of hands for his nails, and wears his teddy-bear trim at 12 mm. GroomSome keeps everything a groomer knows about every animal on one page, so each visit picks up where the last one ended. Owners notice. So do the animals.

AI for your business

Everyone's talking about AI. We make it work for you.

You hear about it everywhere. But where does it actually help in your business, and where do you even start? We find the places where AI gives you time back, build it into the way you already work, and make sure your team is comfortable using it. Whether you work on your own or with hundreds of people.

What it could look like for you

The inbox, sorted before your first coffee.

Routine questions answered in your tone, the rest neatly sorted, so the morning starts with what actually needs you.

Answers at 11 at night, in your own words.

Customers get help the moment they need it, from an assistant that knows your products and your way of doing things.

Your own documents, finally answering back.

Ask a question and get the answer from your manuals, contracts and notes, instead of searching for half an hour.

How we get you there.

Find where it helps.

We look at your day-to-day and find the work AI can genuinely take off your plate. It's often not where you'd expect. And sometimes the honest answer is: not yet.

Build it in, safely.

Connected to the tools you already use. Your data stays yours, and the costs are predictable before you commit.

Make it stick.

We help your team actually use it, so it becomes part of how you work instead of a demo that's forgotten next month.

We build AI into our own products every day. Stickio lets Claude, ChatGPT and Siri help run your week, and Tapinto lets AI assistants share what they've built. That experience goes straight into your project.

We start at the finish line.

Most projects start with a solution: an app, a platform, a bit of AI. We start with one question. What do you want to be different? Then we work backwards. Tools and frameworks are the result of the goal, never the reason for it.

Working backwards, in practice

  1. You say “We need an app.”
  2. We ask “What should be different once you have it?”
  3. The real goal “Fewer customers who forget their appointment.”
  4. What we build Whatever gets you there. Maybe an app. Maybe a friendly reminder that arrives at exactly the right moment. Either way, you'll know why.

And when the answer is software, we take it all the way: designed with the people who'll use it, built to last, and looked after long after launch.

Hoofdpijndossier Charity platform

More than €100,000 raised for one child's surgery abroad.

Hoofdpijndossier was set up to help children with chronic headaches and rare conditions. It brought people together around one child who needed life-changing treatment abroad.

Not every project we're proud of is a product. This one is a reminder that software is only worth what it changes for the people on the other side of the screen.

hoofdpijn-dossier.nl

Started by someone who asks why before how.

Mark Foppen · Founder

Years of building software, for large organisations and for products of his own making, taught Mark one thing above all: technology is never the point. What matters is what changes for the people who use it.

So every project starts with listening. Where do you want to be? What would make the day easier for your team, or your customers? Mark is just as happy to point out a simpler route, or no new software at all, when that gets you there sooner.
