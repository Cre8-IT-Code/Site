About Cre8-it

We start with people. Not with technology.

Cre8-it is a Dutch software company. We make our own products and help businesses get where they want to be. This is how we think, and what drives us.

What years of building taught us.

We've been building software for years: for large organisations, for young startups and for ourselves. Across all of it, one thing stood out. Projects rarely struggled for lack of technology. They struggled when the solution was picked before anyone asked where they wanted to end up.

So we start at the other end: we sit down with the people involved, understand what they want to be different, and work backwards from there.

Sometimes that leads to a full platform. Sometimes to a small automation that gives a team hours back every week. And sometimes to the honest advice not to build anything yet. People remember that kind of candour.

We make our own products the same way: for groomers who care about every animal, runners who just want to run, families keeping their week together and developers who want to share what they make. Each one starts with someone's day.

All the depth. None of the complexity.

Knowing how to build almost anything is what makes it possible to recommend building less.

Software that grows with you

Built on foundations that scale, so success never turns into a problem and costs stay predictable. We are Microsoft Certified (Azure Solutions Architect).

Peace of mind, built in

Your data and your customers' data are protected from day one, not patched up before launch. We are GitHub Advanced Security certified.

AI that earns its place

We build AI into our own products every day, and help businesses of any size find where it genuinely saves time, then make it part of how the team works.

One partner, start to finish

From the first conversation to a product that is live and looked after. No hand-offs, no lost context, no one asking you to explain it all again.

Lessons, shared openly

We write on re-mark-able.net about what works and what doesn't, so others can skip the hard way.

What guides every decision

Start at the finish line.

Know where you want to end up, then work backwards. The tools follow from the goal, never the other way round.

Build what fits.

Not the biggest solution, not the trendiest. The one that matches the problem, the budget and the people using it.

Make it worth it.

Success is measured in what changes for people: time back, worries gone, a better day.
